Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Scar camouflage is a paramedical tattooing technique that deposits pigment into scar tissue to match your surrounding skin tone. It effectively conceals the colour difference between scarred and healthy skin, making scars blend in naturally. The procedure takes 1-3 sessions depending on the scar's size, depth, and type.

Most clients describe the sensation as mild discomfort. We use a topical numbing cream before and during the procedure to ensure your comfort. Sensitivity levels vary by person and treatment area, but our artists work at a gentle pace to minimise any discomfort.

Most clients require 1-3 sessions, spaced 6-8 weeks apart. The number depends on the size, depth, and colour of the scar or stretch mark. During your free consultation, our artist will assess your skin and provide a personalised treatment plan.

Results typically last 2-3 years before a touch-up may be desired. The longevity depends on factors such as your skin type, sun exposure, and aftercare. We recommend a maintenance session every 1-2 years to keep the results looking fresh.

Ideal candidates include anyone with mature scars (at least 12 months old) or white/silver stretch marks. The treatment works on all skin types and tones. We recommend a free consultation to assess your specific case — certain scars and skin conditions may require special consideration.

Healing typically takes 2-4 weeks. The treated area may appear darker initially, then gradually lighten to match your skin tone over the following weeks. We provide detailed aftercare instructions including avoiding sun exposure, swimming, and heavy sweating during healing. The final result is visible after 4-6 weeks when the pigment has fully settled.
